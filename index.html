About

A developer who turns ideas into useful products.

Get to know the person behind the work — and what continues to drive me.

I am a dedicated full stack developer with a strong foundation in both software and hardware. I graduated with an Ordinary Diploma in Information Technology Engineering from the Dar es Salaam Institute of Technology, and I am now completing a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering at the same institution.

I build responsive, user-friendly applications across the stack. My day-to-day work includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, Node.js, Next.js, PHP, and databases such as MySQL and Supabase. I also explore artificial intelligence as part of delivering faster, more useful products.

My path has included internships at Bizy Tech Limited and MUA Insurance Tanzania, web development for Amani Warehouse Limited, and a dual role as entertainment manager and web developer at King Empire. I now work as a software engineer at OpusFesta, a wedding planning platform helping couples in Tanzania plan and preserve their most important day.

When I am not shipping software, I stay close to the Tanzanian developer community, keep learning new tools, and help organize events that bring people together.
